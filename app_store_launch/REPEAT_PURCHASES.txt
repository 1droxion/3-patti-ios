3 Patti Social V1.8.2 — Repeat chip purchases

Chip packs are CONSUMABLE Apple In-App Purchases.
The app does not impose a cumulative purchase count or chip purchase limit.
A player can complete a chip-pack purchase, then purchase the same pack again.
Each successful Apple transaction has a unique transaction ID and is credited once by the backend.
Apple may independently apply payment authorization, fraud prevention, purchase, account, parental, or regional restrictions. The app cannot and must not bypass those Apple controls.

IMPORTANT: Code cannot activate products in App Store Connect. The exact product IDs must exist and be fully configured there before TestFlight/App Store can return them.
